# Documented ranking factors: eBay Best Match and Etsy search (scope `ranking_factors`)

Prepared 2026-10-04. Research only: I scraped no marketplace listing or search pages (I read policy/help/announcement pages only). Cross-checks use files other agents already collected (`parsed.json`, `mine.json`, `itm/*.json`) and are labelled MEASURED.

## 0. Bottom line and ranked actions

Both platforms publish short factor lists and say nothing about weights. What is documented is mostly relevance (title, specifics, tags), listing quality (photos, completeness), buyer behaviour (sales, engagement), the seller's service record, and price/shipping terms. Nothing documented rewards a seller for being "new" except Etsy's small, temporary per-listing boost. eBay does not document any new-listing boost. Because no weights are published, the documentation cannot by itself "solve" the ranking; and the collected eBay result pages include promoted cards (about 11% of cards, roughly 3 of the top 10 in the two queries where they were detected; section 6), so rank patterns there mix ads with organic order.

Ranked by likely impact on placement (documentation first, my inference second):

1. **eBay: remove duplicate fixed-price listings.** About 14 plant/size combinations are live twice (2 exact copies, ~12 cross-format pairs; ~28 of 131 listings; section 2.3). eBay's policy says duplicates are not allowed and lists "one fixed price listing" for identical items, or one listing with variations such as size, among the rules that "help achieve the best possible position in eBay's Best Match sort order". Historical eBay statements say violators lose visibility across their whole inventory.
2. **eBay: raise listing quality to competitor level.** Owner item pages show median 2 photos (n=13) against 6 for competitors (n=14). The four older-format pages have 1 photo, no Genus or Common Name specific, and two of them share an identical MPN. Photo quality and completeness are named Best Match inputs; item specifics are named in eBay's optimization advice and its Seller Center page.
3. **eBay: protect the seller record.** Below Standard means "lower search placement". Dead-on-arrival claims, late shipping and seller cancellations are the realistic ways a plant seller gets there.
4. **eBay: concentrate sales and watchers on one live listing per plant/size.** Use quantity, keep it Good 'Til Cancelled, revise in place, never end and relist to "refresh". "How popular the item is" is a named factor.
5. **Etsy: meet the documented "priority placement" criteria.** First photo at least 2000 px, specific category, a return policy (even "no returns"), shop sections complete, US domestic shipping under $6 (or calculated shipping, or the free-shipping guarantee), service metrics above the floor, title under 15 words that names the item first, all 13 tags as multi-word phrases.
6. **Do not spend effort on myths:** Star Seller badge, Etsy Ads, renewing, video and "add to cart" are documented as NOT changing Etsy's ordering. Relisting and daily ending have no eBay documentation behind them.
7. **Plants are a compliance business first.** Arizona wants an inspection certificate on plants sold into it, Hawaii wants soil-free plants plus inspection, California wants a CDFA nursery licence even for online sales, and Etsy's 2025 Creativity Standards may exclude resold plants. Section 4.

Questions only the owner can answer: (a) do you grow or propagate the plants, or resell wholesale plants (Etsy)? (b) for each duplicate pair, which listing holds the sales and watchers? (c) do you hold a CDFA nursery licence and any county compliance agreement?

### 2.2 New-seller and new-listing dynamics

* **Do new listings get a boost?** Not documented by eBay (Best Match pages list no freshness factor; "Newly listed" is only a sort option). Community and vendor posts claim a "bump" [anecdotal]. MEASURED hint: "JUST LISTED" cards are 3.7% of ranks 1-20 vs 0.7% of ranks 41-60 and about 0.2% beyond (30 cards of 2,353, 23 queries); consistent with a short-lived exposure boost but confounded (INFERENCE).
* **Do Good 'Til Cancelled listings with sales history win?** GTC extends automatically: "When the EndDate is reached, the items EndDate is extended by another 30 days automatically" and "The item number remains the same... throughout their lifetime" [E10, OFFICIAL]. Sales history and watchers staying with that item number is documented by trade/vendor sources, not by an eBay help page [SECONDARY]. Because "How popular the item is" is a named factor, an aged listing with sales carries a real advantage over a fresh clone; the size of that edge is undocumented.
* **Does ending and relisting hurt?** Community reports (consistent, not official): Relist and Sell Similar create a new item number with views, watchers and "sold" counts reset, whereas a GTC rollover keeps its number [ANECDOTAL/SECONDARY]. No eBay source says relisting restores freshness. Revise the live listing instead.
* **How does a seller with 4 feedback compete?** No minimum feedback count appears in eBay's Best Match text; the input is "track record", measured by defects and late shipments. Evaluation uses 12 months of data below 400 transactions, run on the 20th of each month [E5]. On the Canadian policy page, Below Standard requires defects tied to "more than 4 different buyers" [E5]; I could not read the US page for that rule. Third-party guides say new sellers start at Above Standard; eBay's US page does not state it. MEASURED (23 plant queries, logged out, ship-to 94104, 2,353 cards; ads not flagged in this snapshot, see section 6): sellers with at most 10 feedback hold 8.7% of top-10 slots (20 of 230; 7 are the owner's own) vs 12.3% of ranks 11-60; sellers above 1,000 feedback hold 42.6% vs 38.4%. A mild skew toward established sellers, not a wall. The owner has a listing in the top 10 for 7 of 23 queries (ranks 1, 3, 5, 5, 6, 7, 10).

### 2.3 Duplicate-listing policy and the owner's listings

**Policy (OFFICIAL, [E6], read 2026-10-04, undated).** "We don't allow more than one fixed price listing of an identical item at the same time from the same seller. This includes: Listing an identical item in different categories or listing an identical item using different usernames; Listings that aren't significantly different, such as adding an inconsequential bonus item with the same identical item in two listings." Purpose: "no one seller can dominate search results." The page continues: "The following rules help achieve the best possible position in eBay's Best Match sort order", followed by "List identical items in one fixed price listing using eBay bulk listing" and "List items with variations such as size and color in one fixed price listing with variations." Stated actions: "Removing the listing or other content, issuing a warning, restricting activity or account suspension." The search-manipulation policy repeats it: "List identical items in one multi-quantity fixed price listing" [E7].

**Visibility sanction (SECONDARY, historical).** eBay, 1 Jul 2015: "If a seller violates the duplicate listings policy, then all the listings from that seller - including those across linked accounts - will have reduced visibility. This visibility reduction will last until the duplicate listings are removed" (ChannelX describes it for eBay.com; eBay UK/IE community post carries the same text) [E12]. 7 Aug 2018 (UK): "eBay will be demoting sellers in Best Match who have duplicate listings and this will be applied to their whole inventory, not just on duplicated items" [E12]. The current help page names removal/warning/restriction only; whether the whole-inventory Best Match demotion still runs on ebay.com is NOT documented today. Treat it as a credible risk, not a proven current penalty.

**What counts as "different".** Different title wording, price or shipping on the same plant in the same pot size is not a documented difference; different pot size is (variations by "size" are the sanctioned way). The owner's quantity on at least one duplicated listing already reads "More than 10 available", so a second listing adds nothing.

**Owner's listings (MEASURED from `mine.json`, n=131; full candidate list in `SCRATCH/data/ranking_factors_owner_dup_candidates.csv`):**

* Two title formats: 61 "pipe" titles at $6.99 shipping (USPS Ground Advantage), 67 "- Live Houseplant - N-inch Pot" titles at $9.99 (USPS Priority), 3 other at $6.99.
* **Exact copies (same title, price, shipping, different item ids): 2 pairs** - Satin Pothos 4 in ($16.99 + $6.99; ids 168578976270, 168578976237) and ZZ Plant 2 in ($12.99 + $6.99; ids 168578976271, 168578976252).
* **Same plant, same stated pot size in both formats: 8 pairs** - Grey Star, Curly Sue, Hoya Carnosa, Creamsicle, Hoya Kerrii, Syngonium Batik, Monstera Peru 3 in, Syngonium Pink Splash. Older-format total (price + shipping) is $1.55-$7.05 higher than the newer one in every pair. Three more older-format Christmas Cactus colour listings sit against one generic newer listing (probably not identical).
* **4 further likely pairs where the older title omits the size or uses shorthand** (Hoya Sunrise, Rainbow Anthurium, Moonshine, Marble Queen).
* 5 same-name pairs at different pot sizes (e.g. Peperomia Hope 4 in vs 6-inch): not duplicates by size, but they compete for the same query.
* Net: about 14 duplicated plant/size combinations, roughly 28 listings (21%). Some matches are by shorthand name, so confirm by eye.
* The SERP data does not collapse them: one seller can hold several slots in one query (e.g. "monstera peru": ranks 33, 36, 43; "raven zz plant": 27, 76, 82, 114) and 8 of 22 queries with an owner listing show two or more. A listing appearing in search does not mean it is exempt from the policy.

**Practical rule.** For each pair keep the item number that has the sales, watchers and age (check Seller Hub); revise its title, specifics and photos to the better of the two; end the other and do not relist it. If both are zero-history, keep the newer title style (it carries Genus/Common Name) and fix the shipping to one deliberate price. Merge different pot sizes of one plant into a single size-variation listing only if the pots truly are one product (eBay prohibits mixing different products in one listing) [E7].

### 2.5 Plant-category specifics (eBay)

* **Policy [E8, read raw 2026-10-04].** "Plants or seeds prohibited by government or shipping regulations are not allowed." "Endangered plants are not allowed"; "Plants and seeds located outside the U.S. are not allowed due to U.S. import restrictions." Sellers must check the USDA Plants for Planting Manual, USDA Save Our Citrus, and National Plant Board noxious-weed and laws pages. Enforcement can be removal, warning, restriction or suspension. I found no 2025-2026 change to this policy.
* **State restrictions cannot be switched off per state.** eBay's shipping-exclusion list for the US site offers "Alaska/Hawaii", "US Protectorates (including... Puerto Rico, and U.S. Virgin Islands)" and "Army Post Office/Fleet Post Office"; individual states such as Arizona are not listed [E17, OFFICIAL]. A community thread repeats "Alaska and Hawaii is the only states you can block." If you do not want to serve a state, say so in the listing and expect that a cancelled order is a seller-initiated defect (the Canadian policy page lists seller cancellations among transaction defects [E5]). Safer is to comply with the destination state (section 4).
* **Photos and data actually on the owner's pages (MEASURED, n=13 owner item pages vs 14 competitor pages).** Older-format pages: 1 photo each (4 of 4), no Genus or Common Name specific, and identical Soil Type "Loam", Soil pH "Neutral" and Item Weight "2 lb" on all four (looks templated: INFERENCE); MPN values are random-looking strings, and MPN THA9FTFJ is shared by Monstera Peru 3-inch and Peperomia Hope 6-inch. Newer-format pages: 2-4 photos (median 2), Genus and Common Name present. Competitor pages: median 6 photos.
* **Delivery windows (MEASURED, as of 2026-10-04, to ZIP 94104).** Newer listings (Ground Advantage) show "Oct 8 to Oct 16" (up to 12 days for a CA-to-CA parcel); older listings (Priority) show "Oct 7 to Oct 10". Competitor windows in the sample run from Oct 7-10 (ID) to Oct 10-17 (IN). Shorter handling, and Priority for live plants, narrows the window; handling time is a named factor.
* **Reviews and defects.** Under Money Back Guarantee, buyers can claim "not as described"; community reports say eBay generally sides with the buyer on dead plants and there is no "arrived perished" return reason. A claim closed without seller resolution becomes a transaction defect [E5]. Severe-weather protections cover declared events: late-shipment and tracking metrics, and Item Not Received cases where tracking was uploaded and the carrier scanned the parcel inside handling time [E18, excerpt]; they do not cover plant death. So: refund proactively on damaged arrivals, photograph packing, and hold shipments when the destination forecast is extreme.

### 3.2 New-seller and new-listing dynamics

* **New listings:** yes, a small temporary boost so Etsy can learn engagement; Etsy adds that renewing or creating listings only to get the boost "isn't an effective search optimization strategy" [T2].
* **New shops:** the Etsy mythbusting transcript answers the myth "New shops get more priority in Etsy search than old shops" in substance as follows: the Etsy speaker says "we have no information about how shoppers are going to interact with that listing because it's brand new to Etsy", so the listing gets a short boost to learn from, and new listings from established shops are treated the same way [T9, search-tool excerpt and paraphrase]. A search-tool summary of another Etsy handbook page words it as boosting "new shops and new listings... for a very brief time"; I could not tie that line to a page, so I rely on the per-listing wording. Net: a per-listing boost, not a per-shop advantage.
* **No reviews:** Etsy handbook excerpts (search tool) say new listings "will still be shown in search even if they don't have any reviews or ratings yet" as long as there are no other quality issues, and that completed About and Policies sections help placement [Etsy handbook excerpts; same message in T3].
* **How to compete with few reviews:** complete shop sections, meet the service floors from order one, and make the first photo and title do the work. The review-rating floor is an absolute count (at most 4 reviews of 3 stars or lower while under 40 reviews), so a few early bad reviews will not breach it, though they do drag the average rating that Etsy lists as a ranking input.
* **Renewals and history:** a renewal gets a smaller boost than a new listing; do not use it as a strategy. Vendor guides say renewal keeps a listing's sales history, reviews and quality score [ANECDOTAL], so keep and renew a listing that already converts rather than recreating it.

### 3.3 Duplicate-listing policy (Etsy)

* **No Etsy rule against same-shop duplicates was found.** Etsy's Seller Policy bars "duplicate shops" and "any other action (such as manipulating clicks, carts or sales) for the purpose of shilling, manipulating search or circumventing Etsy's policies" [T14, excerpt]. Seller guides say multiple listings of one product are allowed if you can fulfil all the orders; one-of-a-kind items cannot be listed twice [ANECDOTAL].
* **The ranking cost is documented, though:** Frequency Capping [T1] means two listings of the same plant compete for the same query and the shop's top slots are rationed. Identical titles and tags on both read as spam in seller reports [ANECDOTAL].
* **Practical rule:** one listing per plant. Use variations for pot size and quantity for stock. Where you want several listings, differentiate real attributes (size, specimen, bundle), not title wording.

### 3.5 Plant-category specifics (Etsy)

* **Plants, Herbs, Seeds and Soil policy [T13, excerpts].** Etsy disallows plants subject to legal restriction (USDA noxious weeds, CITES, US Endangered Species Act); "it is the seller's responsibility to determine whether the plants... may be legally sold or transported"; "some sales to and from states and territories like Hawaii and Puerto Rico may be restricted". Sales into the US must be US-origin plants.
* **Creativity Standards [T12].** Announced July 16 2024: sellers categorise items as Made by, Designed by, Sourced by or Handpicked by a seller. The June 2025 update added "Resold items from nature" as disallowed (example: bulk landscape rocks resold from a hardware store), and items from nature under "Handpicked" must be "found or cultivated" by the shop; plant starts are an Etsy example of qualifying items [Etsy help excerpt, Listadum]. If the owner grows or propagates plants, say so in the listing's "how it's made" details; if the owner resells wholesale plants unchanged, removal risk is plausible (INFERENCE from the "resold items from nature" wording). Confirm the exact current wording in Etsy's Creativity Standards page, which I could not open.
* **Weather and reviews.** Etsy scores average rating, on-time shipping with tracking and refunded cases; Etsy gives less automatic weather relief than eBay (VAR reported deferred billing and Star Seller help for declared storms only). Add a visible DOA policy (photos within 24 hours is the common wording among plant shops), set realistic processing times, and use Priority Mail in extreme weather.
* **Geo-limits.** Several plant shops list excluded states (HI, AK, PR, CA, AZ and others) in their policies [ANECDOTAL]; I could not read Etsy's own region-block behaviour (community thread requires login).

## 5. Seasonality and weather practice (affects reviews and defect rates)

All of this is ANECDOTAL industry practice unless noted. Shippo's guide (2026-06-15): ship Monday or Tuesday; "Include a heat pack in the package" in cold weather; below 50 F warrants heat protection and above 85 F needs cooling; "Ground shipping works only for nearby zones with 1-2 day transit"; Priority Mail for 1-3 days. Plant-shop sellers on Etsy report adding heat packs when destination lows approach the high 30s F, using Priority Mail only in cold months, and asking for photos within 24 hours for DOA claims. Another guide advises against shipping when lows are forecast below 20 F even with a heat pack. Today is 2026-10-04: cold-weather shipping season starts for much of the country, while the Inland Empire can still run hot. Practical rules for the owner: use Priority rather than Ground Advantage for plants in either temperature extreme; ship Monday-Wednesday; check the forecast at the destination ZIP, not just origin; offer heat packs as an add-on and write a plain DOA policy into every listing.
