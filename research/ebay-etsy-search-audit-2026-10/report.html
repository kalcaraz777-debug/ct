What eBay says it uses

eBay's own Best Match page lists: how closely the listing matches the search, how popular the item is, price, listing quality (photos and description), how complete the listing is (item specifics), terms such as returns and handling time, and your track record as a seller. It publishes no weights. Shipping cost isn't on the list, and in our data free shipping alone barely mattered (+0.04). Total delivered price did matter (−0.23).

What this means for you

You already pass the first gate: your titles match. But 85% of the 621 listings ranked above your best ones also contain every search word, so matching alone isn't enough. The tie-breakers you can influence are popularity (watchers, sales, carts), listing quality (photos, a full description), freshness, and a clean catalog with one listing per plant.

Fix list

What to change, in order

Clean the catalog this week

  1. End one listing from each of the 15 duplicate pairs (table below). Keep the one with sales or watchers. If neither has any, keep the newer format, which is $1.55–$7.05 cheaper delivered and has better item specifics. eBay's duplicate-listings policy allows one fixed-price listing per identical item.
  2. Fix descriptions that describe the wrong plant. "Shooting Star" describes Dodecatheon meadia, a woodland garden perennial. "Siltepecana El Salvador" calls itself a Hoya. The two "Creamsicle" listings disagree on what the plant is. The old "Grey Star" description calls it a Hoya, while your newer Grey Star is a Calathea, so one of them is wrong. Check each against the plant you actually ship.
  3. Remove the "add Thermal Cool Shield / Heat Pack" lines from your eBay descriptions, or create an eBay heat-pack listing. All 64 newer descriptions tell buyers to add one, but it only exists in your Etsy shop.
  4. Put care instructions and the live-arrival guarantee back. Your older template had both in 67 of 67 listings. The newer template dropped them in 64 of 64.
  5. Set handling to 1 business day if you can ship that fast. eBay names handling time as a Best Match input, and it shortens the delivery estimate buyers see.

Raise listing quality on the plants you can win next 2 weeks

  1. Shoot 5–8 real photos per listing, starting with the Defend and Reachable plants. Cover the whole plant, a leaf close-up, a top-down view, the pot and roots, a hand for scale, and the packed box. The 6 first photos we inspected were cut-outs on an artificial backdrop with a logo; eBay's photo guidance says not to add borders or text.
  2. Revise these listings in place; don't end and relist. Revising keeps the item number, watchers and sales count, and it makes the listing "recently revised" like 59% of the unpaid top 3.
  3. Rename the vague older titles (20 suggestions below). Each one is built only from words in your own description, so confirm the plant before using it.
  4. Fix delivered price on the older listings. They average $33.28 delivered against $24.98 for the newer format, mostly from $9.99 shipping. Free shipping didn't move rank by itself, but delivered price did. If you fold shipping into the price, keep the total at or below today's newer-format total.

Build buyer activity, then test ads ongoing

  1. Concentrate demand. With one listing per plant, every watcher and sale lands on the same item number. Use eBay's "Send offers to buyers" for listings with watchers, and volume pricing ("buy 2, save 5–10%"). Volume pricing is already set on your Variegated Ficus.
  2. Run a small Priority (cost-per-click) Promoted Listings test on the Defend and Reachable plants, but only after the photos and descriptions are fixed. An ad sends clicks to the listing, so a weak listing wastes the budget. Since January 2026 only Priority campaigns can win the ad slot just under the top result.
  3. On Etsy: cut titles to 15 words or fewer, name the plant first, and drop "from Riverside Greenhouses" (your shop name already shows). Move "Easy Care", "Pet Friendly" and similar into tags, and fill all 13 tags with multi-word phrases. Add the 4 missing plants. Use a first photo at least 2,000 px wide. Say in each listing that you grow or propagate the plant yourself: Etsy's 2025 Creativity Standards allow plants the shop cultivates but not plants resold unchanged.
  4. Ask every happy Etsy buyer for a review. Review count is the biggest visible gap between you and the shops that keep appearing (35 vs thousands).

How this was measured

Method and limits

  • eBay searches: 25 plant searches, Best Match, logged out, ship-to ZIP 94104, captured twice on 4 Oct 2026 (2,057 result cards). The two captures agreed closely (rank correlation 0.86–1.00), so the order is stable day to day. Ads were identified from the rendered "Sponsored" label. Results personalise by buyer history and location, so your own view may differ.
  • eBay listings: 292 item pages read in full, covering all 131 of yours, the top 3 for every search (ads and unpaid separately), and 45 mid- and low-ranked listings as a comparison group. 18 seller feedback profiles and store pages.
  • Etsy: no direct access (bot protection). Google search and Google Shopping results, plus Etsy's public keyword pages, as described above.
  • Ranking factors: eBay and Etsy help pages, announcements and reputable trade press. Neither platform publishes weights.
  • Limits: one day of data, 25 searches, correlation rather than proof. A planned test of how a far-away ship-to ZIP changes rank was not run.
